# Review Recovery Checklist

## 1) Profile audit
- Claim and verify Google Business Profile
- Check hours, services, photos, and FAQs
- Identify stale or incomplete information

## 2) Review gap analysis
- Compare review count vs competitors in the same area
- Flag old or generic reviews
- Review response speed and tone

## 3) Response templates
- Positive review: thank, mention specifics, invite future contact
- Neutral review: acknowledge, clarify, invite a private follow-up
- Negative review: apologize, fix the issue, move to a direct channel

## 4) Review request workflow
- Ask satisfied customers after service completion
- Keep script short and natural
- Share an easy link to the review form

## 5) 14-day action plan
- Day 1-3: fix profile basics
- Day 4-7: collect fresh reviews
- Day 8-14: respond consistently and improve trust signals
