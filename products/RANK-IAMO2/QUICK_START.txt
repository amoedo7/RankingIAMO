INVOICE PRO – QUICK START GUIDE

1. OPEN THE TEMPLATE
   - Unzip this package
   - Open 'InvoicePro_Template.gsheet' in Google Drive
   - Click 'Make a copy' to your own Drive

2. CUSTOMIZE YOUR HEADER
   - Replace [YOUR COMPANY] with your business name
   - Add your company address, phone, email
   - Insert your logo (optional)

3. CREATE AN INVOICE
   - Enter invoice number (auto-fills if you use the formula)
   - Set issue date and due date
   - Add line items: description, quantity, rate
   - Tax percentage auto-calculates (adjust if needed)
   - Total populates automatically

4. ADD PAYMENT DETAILS
   - Choose your payment method(s):
     • Bank transfer (IBAN/details)
     • PayPal link
     • Stripe link
     • Crypto wallet
     • CobrAMO payment link
   - Paste in the 'Payment Methods' section

5. EXPORT TO PDF
   - File → Download → PDF Document
   - Ready to send to your client

6. REUSE FOR FUTURE INVOICES
   - Duplicate the invoice sheet for each new client
   - Keep one master template untouched

Tips:
- Use the 'Notes' section for payment terms, references, or project details
- Customize currency and tax rates to match your region
- Add your terms of payment at the bottom
